Frequently asked questions

What is Stackadoo, and where can I get it?

Stackadoo is a tower-stacking game for Android and iOS. Place blocks that have faces, build as high as you can and keep the tower standing through tremors. Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

Is Stackadoo free?

Yes. You can play everything for free and earn coins and power-ups as you go. Optional ads and optional purchases pay for the game.

What does the No Ads Pass do?

Removes banners and pop-up ads (the full-screen ads between runs). Bonus video ads stay. Those are the ones you choose to watch for a free power-up, a revive, doubled coins or a second gift. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. You can restore it on a new phone with the same store account. Power-ups are not included.

How do I buy coins or power-ups, and how do I restore a purchase?

Open the Shop from Home. You can spend coins on single power-ups and skins, or buy coin packs and power-up packs with real money. To restore the No Ads Pass on a new phone, tap “Restore purchases” at the bottom of the Shop, or go to Settings > Purchases > Restore purchases. Restore brings back the No Ads Pass. It does not give back coins or power-ups, so anything you already spent or used stays gone.

I paid, but my purchase hasn’t arrived. What now?

Wait a moment: if the game says “Payment pending. You’ll get it once it’s confirmed.” or “Not confirmed yet. If you were charged, it arrives by itself.”, the store is still confirming it and the item is added automatically. Restart the game with a network connection, then try Restore purchases. Restore tells you what it found: “1 purchase restored”, “Nothing to restore” or “Couldn’t reach the store. Try again.” If it’s still missing after a day, write to me at mkhakpaki.ksas@gmail.com with the order number from your store receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store, not by me. Request one through your store account’s order history. I can’t issue refunds myself.

Why do I see ads, and how do I change my privacy choices?

Stackadoo shows three kinds of ads. Video ads are your choice. A small banner sits at the bottom of most screens. And you may see a full-screen ad when you restart a run, never in the middle of one. In some regions (such as the EU, UK and some US states) a privacy form asks for your choice when the game first shows it, usually near the start, and Settings > Privacy choices reopens it. If you don’t see that row, your region doesn’t need it. The No Ads Pass removes the banners and the full-screen ads.

I changed phones or reinstalled. Is my progress gone?

Stackadoo has no account and no cloud save. Your progress (best height, coins, power-ups, streak, skins) is saved on your phone only. If your phone backs up app data, reinstalling may bring your progress back. Otherwise it doesn’t move to a new phone by itself. Purchases made with your store account can be restored (see “How do I buy coins or power-ups, and how do I restore a purchase?”).

When does the Daily tower change?

Every day. The Daily screen shows how long until the next tower. You get one free try a day, plus one retry after watching an ad if you missed the goal.

What happens to my Daily streak if I miss a day?

The streak counts the days in a row that you play the Daily tower, up to day 7, then it starts over at day 1. Every day of the streak pays a reward: coins, a power-up or both. If you miss a day, the streak starts again at day 1. Sometimes, after a good streak, the game offers “Keep my streak” for watching an ad. That only works for a single missed day, and it is not always on offer.

Which languages does Stackadoo speak, and how do I switch?

English and Persian. Go to Settings > Language and pick one. Persian reads right to left, with Persian digits.

Can I turn off sound, haptics or screen shake?

Yes. In Settings, under “Sound & feel”, switch off Sound effects, Music, Haptics or “Reduce motion and flashes” (which also turns off screen shake and flashing lights). Sound, Music and Haptics are in the Pause menu too. “Block faces”, in the Game section, hides the faces on the blocks.

How do I contact you?

Use the Contact section below: it lists what to send me.

Why can’t I watch an ad for a power-up?

If a button says “No video right now”, no ad is ready on your phone. Try again in a moment, or buy one with coins instead. Video ads are always optional.

Who is Stackadoo for?

Stackadoo is made for players aged 13 and over.

How do I delete my data?

Everything about your game lives on your phone. Deleting the game removes it, and you can delete any backup your phone made in your phone’s settings. For the data my service providers hold (crash reports, analytics, ads and purchases), see Delete your data.

What is the Free gift?

Tap Free gift on Home to collect a free power-up. You can open one free gift a day. If you want, watch an ad for a second one. The gifts come back every day, and the screen shows how long until the next.

I ran out of hearts. Can I keep going?

When your last heart goes, you can watch an ad and carry on with a heart back. You get one revive per run. If the ad doesn’t load, the button says “No video right now”; try again in a moment, or end the run.

A Buy button says “Store not available”.

The store couldn’t be reached, so the button is greyed out. Check your connection and try again later. If a message says “Something went wrong. You weren’t charged.”, nothing was bought.

How do I see a power-up’s how-to again?

Long-press any power-up in a run, or go to Settings > Power-up tips > Show again.
