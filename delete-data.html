Delete your data

Where your Stackadoo data lives, and how to delete each part. This page is honest about what I can and cannot find.

To ask for deletion of any Stackadoo data: e-mail mkhakpaki.ksas@gmail.com (Stackadoo, by Mohammad Khakpaki) with the subject "Delete my Stackadoo data". Include your Support ID (Settings ▸ Help & legal ▸ Support ID; tap it to copy). Say which of the parts below you mean. If you have bought something, you can also include your store order or transaction number. I answer within one month. What can and cannot be found is explained below.

On your phone

Your progress, coins, power-ups, skins, settings and purchase records are stored on your phone only. To delete them, delete the game. On Android you can instead go to Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Stackadoo ▸ Storage and tap Clear data.

If your phone backs up app data (Android backup, iCloud), a reinstall may restore it. Delete the backup in your phone's settings if you want it gone. Deleting the game does not delete a Play Games cloud save (see below) or your purchases from the store. After reinstalling, Restore can bring back the No Ads Pass, but not used coins or power-ups.

What your Support ID reaches

The Support ID is a random code the game makes on your phone (see the Privacy Policy). Send it to me and I will delete what is linked to it:

  • your Adapty profile;
  • your Firebase Analytics data, using Google's User Deletion API;
  • the Crashlytics reports linked to it, where Firebase's tools let me (they are deleted automatically after 90 days in any case);
  • the e-mails you sent me that carry it.

The code does not reach Google's own ad data, Google Signals data linked to your Google account, or anything sent before the game had a Support ID, or while analytics consent was not granted (no Support ID is attached to analytics then). For those, use the steps below and Google's own controls.

Some records may have to be kept where the law requires. Deleting the game and clearing its data gives you a new code, so keep your old one if you want to ask later.

Ads

  • Change your consent: open Settings ▸ Privacy choices in the game, where it is shown, and change your answer.
  • Android advertising ID: reset or delete it in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads (the exact path varies by phone).
  • iPhone and iPad: in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking, turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or switch off tracking for Stackadoo.
  • Google's ad settings: manage or turn off personalised ads at myadcenter.google.com.

Ad data held by Google is kept under Google's own policies. I have no way to look up or delete it for you.

Analytics and crash data

Firebase keeps crash reports for 90 days, then deletes them automatically. Analytics data linked to your installation is deleted automatically 14 months after the last time you play; totals that identify no one, such as daily counts, are kept longer. To delete data linked to you, send me your Support ID (see above). Data not linked to it, such as totals that identify no one, cannot be deleted for one person.

Uninstalling the game stops any new data from being sent. You can also reset your advertising ID (see above), which breaks the link between old and new ad-related data.

More in the Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy page.

Google Play Games

If you used Play Games on Android, Google holds your leaderboard scores, achievements and cloud save. I do not hold them and cannot delete them. To delete them, open the Play Games app, go to your profile and its settings, and choose Delete Play Games account & data. You can also delete the data for this game alone there. See Google's help.

Deleting the game removes your save on the phone but does not delete the cloud copy. Your player ID is kept only on your phone, and goes when you delete the game or clear its data. The game has no sign-out button: sign out in the Play Games app.

Notifications

Notifications are available on Android only for now. If you switched on news messages, Google holds a push token for your installation. To delete it, switch off Settings ▸ Notifications in the game. That cancels the reminders, unsubscribes the topics and deletes the token. The game has no separate delete button for notifications.

Turning notifications off in your phone's settings only stops them being shown. The game does not send the token to me, so I cannot look it up. If you uninstall the game, messages stop, and I do not control when Google removes an old token.

Your reminder times stay on your phone only, and go when you delete the game or clear its data.

Purchases at Adapty

Adapty keeps a purchase profile for your installation, with your Support ID as its customer ID, your purchase history and store country. Your Support ID is the quickest way to find it.

To ask for deletion: e-mail mkhakpaki.ksas@gmail.com with your Support ID. If you can no longer see it, for example after a reinstall, use your store order or transaction number instead:

  • Google Play: the order ID, which starts with GPA. It is in your Google Play receipt e-mail and in Google Play's order history.
  • App Store: the order ID or the receipt from Apple's purchase e-mail.

I will try to find your purchase profile with it, and delete it if I do. Please also tell me your device and store account type. If you have never bought anything, there may be nothing linked to you that I can find. Some records may have to be kept where the law requires, for example for tax and accounting, and I will tell you if that applies. Deleting the profile may mean the No Ads Pass can no longer be restored through Adapty.

Google Play and Apple records

Payments and purchase records are the stores' own, not mine, and I cannot delete them. To manage them, see Google Play's help on order history and receipts and Apple's help on your purchase history.

How fast I answer

I reply to deletion and other privacy requests within one month, the standard under the GDPR. I may ask you for something to confirm the request is yours. If I cannot find any data, I will say so. For the rights you have and how to complain, see Your rights.
