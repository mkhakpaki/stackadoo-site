Delete your data

Where your Stackadoo data lives, and how to delete each part. This page is honest about what I can and cannot find.

To ask for deletion of any Stackadoo data: e-mail mkhakpaki.ksas@gmail.com (Stackadoo, by Mohammad Khakpaki) with the subject "Delete my Stackadoo data". Say which of the parts below you mean. If you have bought something, include your store order or transaction number. I answer within one month. What can and cannot be found is explained below.

On your phone

Your progress, coins, power-ups, skins, settings and purchase records are stored on your phone only. To delete them, delete the game. On Android you can instead go to Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Stackadoo ▸ Storage and tap Clear data.

If your phone backs up app data (Android backup, iCloud), a reinstall may restore it. Delete the backup in your phone's settings if you want it gone. Deleting the game does not delete your purchases from the store. After reinstalling, Restore can bring back the No Ads Pass, but not used coins or power-ups.

Ads

  • Change your consent: open Settings ▸ Privacy choices in the game, where it is shown, and change your answer.
  • Android advertising ID: reset or delete it in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads (the exact path varies by phone).
  • iPhone and iPad: in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking, turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or switch off tracking for Stackadoo.
  • Google's ad settings: manage or turn off personalised ads at myadcenter.google.com.

Ad data held by Google is kept under Google's own policies. I have no way to look up or delete it for you.

Analytics and crash data

Firebase keeps crash reports for 90 days, then deletes them automatically. Analytics data linked to your installation is deleted automatically 14 months after it is collected; totals that identify no one, such as daily counts, are kept longer. The game shows no identifier that links this data to you, so I cannot find your records by name or e-mail, and I cannot delete a single person's analytics or crash data on request.

Uninstalling the game stops any new data from being sent. You can also reset your advertising ID (see above), which breaks the link between old and new ad-related data.

More in the Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy page.

Purchases at Adapty

Adapty keeps a purchase profile for your installation, with an anonymous ID, your purchase history and store country. You cannot see that ID in the game, so to find your profile I need a purchase to look it up by.

To ask for deletion: e-mail mkhakpaki.ksas@gmail.com with your store order or transaction number:

  • Google Play: the order ID, which starts with GPA. It is in your Google Play receipt e-mail and in Google Play's order history.
  • App Store: the order ID or the receipt from Apple's purchase e-mail.

I will try to find your purchase profile with it, and delete it if I do. Please also tell me your device and store account type. If you have never bought anything, there may be nothing linked to you that I can find. Some records may have to be kept where the law requires, for example for tax and accounting, and I will tell you if that applies. Deleting the profile may mean the No Ads Pass can no longer be restored through Adapty.

Google Play and Apple records

Payments and purchase records are the stores' own, not mine, and I cannot delete them. To manage them, see Google Play's help on order history and receipts and Apple's help on your purchase history.

How fast I answer

I reply to deletion and other privacy requests within one month, the standard under the GDPR. I may ask you for something to confirm the request is yours. If I cannot find any data, I will say so. For the rights you have and how to complain, see Your rights.
