Stackadoo: a wobbly tower-stacking game

Build it tall. Watch it wobble.

Stackadoo is a tower-stacking game for your phone. Place blocks that have faces, build as high as you can and keep the tower standing when the ground shakes. Climb past famous landmarks, through the clouds and all the way to space.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

How it plays

Pick, place, take your time

There is no clock on your moves, so take your time.

Step 1

Pick a block

You get three blocks in a tray. Pick any one.

Step 2

Place it

Drag it onto your tower and let go when its outline glows. Use all three and you get three new ones.

Step 3

Keep it standing

If a block falls off, you lose a heart. Lose them all and the run is over, unless you watch an ad to revive. You get one revive per run. Your height only counts once the blocks stop wobbling.

Tremors and gusts

The world pushes back

Tremors

In normal runs the ground shakes now and then. A red card warns you first, and the tray pauses until the shaking stops. Tap Freeze, then your tower, to keep every block in place. Or hold on and see what holds.

Gusts

In the Daily tower the wind picks up instead. A card says which side the gust comes from.

Low gravity

Space has its own surprise. Blocks float and settle slowly.

Landmarks and zones

Measured against real landmarks

Pass one and you get a cheer on screen, some coins and, the first time ever, a free power-up.

Six landmarks

  • Statue of Liberty
  • Big Ben
  • Great Pyramid
  • Eiffel Tower
  • Empire State Building
  • Burj Khalifa

Four zones

The sky changes as you climb.

  • Street level
  • Cloud level
  • Night sky
  • Space

Milestone lines on the way pay coins. When a run ends you can share a picture of your tower, with the landmark you beat (“Taller than Big Ben!”).

The Daily tower

One new tower a day

Everyone gets the same blocks. You have a goal height and a limit on blocks, and one free try a day. If you miss the goal you can try once more after watching an ad. Reach the goal and you earn bonus coins.

Play on consecutive days to build a streak, with a reward for every day of a 7-day streak. Miss a day and the streak starts over, though after a good streak the game may offer to save it for an ad.

Power-ups

Six helpers to get you out of trouble

The Shop, the Free gift, landmarks and ads add more.

  • Undo

    Takes back your last placement. The block goes back to your tray and the tower snaps back, even a lost life.

  • Glue

    Your next block sticks to whatever it touches when you place it. It can’t slide off or tip over.

  • Freeze

    Turns the tower you have now to ice, so nothing in it can slide or fall. Blocks you add on top still move.

  • Hammer

    Tap any block in your tower to smash it out. Great for a wobbly overhang. It never costs a life.

  • Bird’s-eye

    Zooms out to your whole tower. Safe gaps that were out of view glow, so you can place your block in a hole deep inside.

  • Reroll

    Swaps all 3 blocks in your tray for new ones when you’re stuck with shapes that don’t fit.

Each has a limit per run, so every player faces the same rules.

Free to play

Free to play, with optional ads and purchases

Stackadoo is free. You can earn coins and power-ups just by playing, and you can buy more if you want to.

Ads, kept in check

  • Watching a video ad is always your choice. Watch one to get a power-up, to revive once in a run, to double your coins or to open a second gift.
  • Between runs you may see a full-screen ad when you restart, never in the middle of a run. A banner sits at the bottom of most screens.

No Ads Pass

One-time purchase. Removes banners and pop-up ads. Bonus video ads stay.

Coins and packs

Spend coins on power-ups and skins. Coin packs and power-up packs are in the Shop. If you ever need to, Restore purchases brings the No Ads Pass back.

Fair play and privacy in brief

  • No clock on your moves. Take as long as you like to place a block.
  • Video ads are always optional. The No Ads Pass leaves them in, so you can still choose to watch one for a power-up, a revive or extra coins.
  • Power-up limits per run are the same however you got the power-up, whether you earned it, watched for it or bought it.
  • Power-ups are not part of the No Ads Pass. You never have to buy them: you can earn or watch for them.
  • A fair mix of blocks: they come from a shuffled bag, so every shape keeps coming round.
